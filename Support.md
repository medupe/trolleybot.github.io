## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. The app displays a connection or server error. What should I do?
* Verify that your device has an active internet connection via Wi-Fi or cellular data.
* Force close the app and re-open it.
* Ensure you have updated to the latest version of Trolleybot available on the App Store.

### 2. How is my personal data handled?
* We prioritize your security and privacy. You can review how your data is collected and protected by reading our [Privacy Policy](https://medupe.github.io/privacy).
