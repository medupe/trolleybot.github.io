1. Information Collection and Use
Trolleybot is designed to respect your privacy. The app does not require users to create an account, log in, or provide any direct personally identifiable information (such as your name, address, or phone number).

To provide the core functionality of returning supermarket specials, the app processes the following:

Chat Prompts: The text you enter into the chatbot to search for grocery items and products. These prompts are processed in real-time to generate relevant specials from Shoprite, Pick n Pay, and Checkers.

2. Third-Party Services
Trolleybot utilizes third-party services to function securely and efficiently. These services may collect information used to identify your device for security and monitoring purposes.

The app uses the following third-party service providers:

Google Gemini AI: Used to process your chatbot prompts and understand your grocery requests.

Firebase App Check: Used to protect the app and its backend infrastructure from abuse by verifying that incoming traffic comes from the legitimate Trolleybot app.

Firebase AI Logic Monitoring: Used to monitor the performance and reliability of the AI responses.

Google Play Services: Standard services required for Android apps distributed via the Google Play Store.

For more information on how these services handle data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising and Payments
Trolleybot is completely free to use. It does not contain any advertisements, and it does not process or request any payment information.

4. Data Security and Retention
I value your trust in providing us your data, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. Since Trolleybot does not utilize user accounts, your chat prompts are only processed momentarily to fetch your requested grocery specials. We do not store long-term, identifiable user profiles.

5. Children’s Privacy
Trolleybot does not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.
